# CMS ERP Modules for Colleges

This document outlines the essential and expected modules for a college Management Information System (MIS) / Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) platform.

---

## 1. Core Academic & Student Management
* **Student Information System (SIS):** Student profile lifecycle from admission to graduation (personal info, documents, academic history, disciplinary records).
* **Admissions & Enrollment:** Online application portals, document verification, merit list generation, fee collection, and registration.
* **Attendance Management:** Digital tracking (app, RFID, or biometric) of student and staff attendance, auto-calculating eligibility percentages and sending alerts for low attendance.
* **Timetable & Scheduling:** Drag-and-drop engines to schedule classes, labs, and exams without room or faculty conflicts.

## 2. Assessment & Examination
* **Exam Management:** Scheduling, seating plans, invigilator allocation, and hall ticket generation.
* **Grading & Results (OBE compliant):** Internal and external mark entry, GPA/CGPA auto-calculation, moderation workflows, and automated report card/transcript generation. Support for Outcome-Based Education (OBE) mapping (mapping exam questions to Course Outcomes).

## 3. Finance & Operations
* **Fee Management:** Dynamic fee structures (tuition, hostel, library, transport), online payment gateway integration, automated dues notifications, installment plans, and reconciliations.
* **HRMS & Payroll:** Faculty/staff profiles, service logs, biometric integration, leave management, appraisal cycles, and automated payroll processing (PF, tax deductions, pay slips).
* **Inventory & Asset Management:** Purchase orders, vendor management, lab equipment tracking, stock distribution, and department audits.

## 4. Student Life & Services
* **Learning Management System (LMS):** Assignment distribution, online quizzes, study material repository, and discussion boards.
* **Hostel Management:** Room allocation, mess billing, warden/security logs, and gate pass management.
* **Transport Management:** Route mapping, vehicle tracking, driver allocation, and maintenance logs.
* **Library Management:** Cataloging (ISBN/barcode/RFID search), book issuing/returns, fine management, and e-resource access control.
* **Placement Cell (TPO):** Student resume collection, recruiter/company management, eligibility filters, job postings, and drive outcome analysis.

## 5. Administration & Compliance
* **Accreditation & Compliance (e.g., NAAC, NBA, NIRF, ABET):** Generation of regulatory reports, faculty research/publication records, and demographic metrics required for audits.
* **Communication Suite:** Automated WhatsApp, SMS, email, and push notifications for announcements, fee notices, and attendance warnings.
* **Role-based Dashboards (MIS):** Custom views for HODs, Principals, Exam Cells, Finance, and Platform Admins to track real-time analytics.
